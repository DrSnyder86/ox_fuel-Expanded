# Asset Notices

This companion contains original DRS Sentinel geometry: station, connector, portable ground/carry pack, and three docked cable meshes. Distributed under GPL-3.0; keep the included `LICENSE`.

It also contains the original DRS RON style-A pump cabinet and two idle hose meshes. Its branding uses the existing community-provided RON artwork.

The universal `drs_fuel_nozzle` is original DRS geometry and procedurally generated material artwork, distributed under GPL-3.0. Its mounting coordinate frame is compatible with the stock nozzle solely to preserve hand/vehicle offsets and hose endpoints. No stock GTA geometry or textures are redistributed. Original source, export scripts, and validation are in `ox_fuel/development/fuel_nozzle`.

The older CDN-Fuel `electric_charger` / `electric_nozzle` assets have been removed. The original DRS connector retains their mounting coordinate frame solely to preserve user-tuned attachment offsets, not their geometry.

Editable source and reproducible export scripts: `ox_fuel/development/sentinel_ev_station/build_station.py` and `build_sentinel_set.py`.

RON source, embedded texture inputs, and validation: `ox_fuel/development/ron_pump_model/build_ron.py`.
